Principles
Protein with every meal — spread across 4–5 sittings, don't save it all for dinner
Anchor carbs around workouts — before and after. Evening meals can be lower carb
On rest days, pull carbs down ~30–40g. Keep protein the same
Eat within 45 min post-workout — protein + carb combo
Water: 80–100 oz/day. Schedule it — don't wait to feel thirsty
Alcohol directly blunts muscle protein synthesis. Keep to 1–2 occasions/week max
Watch invisible calories — oils, dressings, nuts, drinks add up fast
