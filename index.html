Fitness Plan

Progressive overload: when all reps are clean, add 5 lbs (lower body) or 2.5 lbs (upper body) next session. Log every session.

2 × 12 steps each leg

3 × 15–20 · 3 sec slow eccentric

3 × 10 each side
